# Code of conduct

The rules for behavior in Cartouche's issues, discussions, pull requests and chats. Read by everyone who takes part.

Cartouche adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) by reference. The full text applies. This page is a short summary.

## Summary

- Be respectful. Welcome people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept feedback kindly. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten or discriminate.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Do not post sexual content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not make personal attacks or trolling comments.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, and ban people who break these rules. They apply the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.

Contact: open a private GitHub security advisory or a discussion
