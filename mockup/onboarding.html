Drag Cartouche to Applications. The first time you open it:
  1. macOS says it can’t check Cartouche. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password. Updates won’t ask again.

Cartouche is free and signed with its own certificate, not Apple’s paid notarization. That’s why macOS asks once when you use the disk image. The install script avoids it.
