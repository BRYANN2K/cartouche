## Narrative

I started with the damaged world, then repeated startup with an empty home and an offline home to separate fixture problems from a general startup problem. Cartouche wrote its startup log, but I could not obtain an accessible window through computer use; the QA process remained near one CPU core and the mock recorded no application requests. Independently, the bundled MCP helper rejected initialization because the harness-generated Unix socket path was too long. I verified the bundle metadata, the corrupt-cache rejection log, and the installation wording that could be checked without a working window. I have recorded downstream scenarios as blocked rather than inventing click paths or treating armed flags as completed tests.

## Harness limits

- `qa/README.md` says this debug build does not start Sparkle. The appcast uses placeholder signatures; `runners.json.sig` is absent. Do not treat a lack of an update offer as beta gating or foreign-key refusal. UPD-02/03/04 need an appropriate signed/update-capable test arrangement after startup is usable.
- The mock documents permission/controller/installer/crash/BIOS/iCloud/onboarding flags as **recorded** with effects delegated to other fakes or the QA home. A successful arm response alone is not evidence that Cartouche displayed or enforced that state. No fake TCC interaction was reached, and no real System Settings pane was opened.
- The `stores` profile cannot seed real Keychain sessions; the launcher maps it to the Steam world. Expired Epic was recorded in broken, but sign-in/session-expiry UI was not verified. No real account was opened.
- Steam/RetroArch dmgs and executable payloads are stubs. Actual game rendering, frame measurement, redistributable behavior, netplay, iCloud/GOG synchronization, official RetroArch mounting and real permission persistence remain outside what this run can prove.
- No CI workflow, install script, release signing flow or real website was executed. The referenced `RTK.md` was not present at the supplied workspace/repository locations or searched ancestors; no contents were inferred.
